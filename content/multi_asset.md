The fund is 100% equities by mandate. In a multi-asset portfolio I would pair the same equity convictions with real diversifiers. A starting allocation would be 70% equities (this book), 10% gold, 15% Treasuries and 5% commodities, tilted around the regimes below.

## Gold
The most consistent diversifier over the period: gold crossed $3,000/oz in March 2025, $4,000 in October 2025 and set a record above $5,500 in January 2026, then corrected to below $4,000 by late June 2026. I would hold 10% as a strategic position, add on large pull-backs (June 2026) and never sell it to chase equities.

## Treasuries
Bonds failed as a hedge in the April 2025 tariff shock, when yields rose with equities falling, but worked during the Fed's cutting cycle in late 2025. Since inflation re-accelerated in 2026 and the Fed started hiking in September, I would keep duration short (T-bills, 1–3 year notes) and only extend once inflation is clearly falling.

## Commodities
Oil was the hedge for the 2026 Iran war: up about 50% in the first two weeks, with Brent near $120 at the peak. I would have held a 5% commodity sleeve, taken profits after the April ceasefire, and moved to underweight after the November 2026 midterms as the market started pricing an end to the war.

## Regime summary
- **Risk-on, falling rates** (Apr–Oct 2025): maximum equity weight, keep gold, add duration.
- **Equity sell-off, sticky inflation** (Jan–Mar and Jun–Jul 2026): gold, commodities and cash; short duration.
- **Hawkish Fed, policy risk** (today): gold, short-dated Treasuries, neutral equities with a quality tilt.
