**What happened.** The risk I flagged materialised. At the end of October, disappointing Big Tech results and negative free cash flow from AI capex wiped about $2 trillion off the Nasdaq-100; Alphabet fell 12.6% in a single day. The Fed held rates on 28 October but two members voted to hike. On 5 November, after the Democrats swept the midterms, QQQ fell 6.5% in a day on fears that the new Congress could slow data-centre construction; Nvidia lost about 9% and bitcoin fell below $76,000. On 12 November Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO amid a scandal.

**Why the fund moved like this.** Every pillar of the book was hit at once: AI hardware (NVDA), the power-for-data-centres theme (GEV), AI software (PLTR) and crypto (COIN, MARA). The fund fell roughly twice as much as QQQ, in line with its beta and its concentration in exactly the themes being questioned.

**What I expected next.** I distinguished between what had changed and what had not. The political risk to data-centre permitting is real but slow-moving; the demand for compute and power is not going away. The Huang resignation was the most acute risk for the book, as he is central to Nvidia's strategy and customer relationships.

**Multi-asset view.** Gold held around $4,080/oz; oil fell sharply on bets that the election would speed an end to the Iran war, so I would have cut any remaining energy exposure.
