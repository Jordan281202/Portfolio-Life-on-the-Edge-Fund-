**What happened.** August brought hopes of a deal to reopen shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, and markets steadied. On 16 September the Fed raised rates for the first time since 2023, by 25 bp, signalling that inflation was its priority.

**Why the fund moved like this.** For two months the fund moved in line with QQQ. With no big factor move in either direction, there was no big selection effect either.

**What I expected next.** A hiking Fed is a headwind for long-duration growth stocks, so I did not expect the fund to regain its May highs quickly. The risk I flagged was the third-quarter earnings season: any sign that AI capex was not earning a return would hit the whole AI complex at once.

**Multi-asset view.** Keep fixed income at the short end (front-end yields were attractive after the hike), hold gold, stay neutral on commodities.
