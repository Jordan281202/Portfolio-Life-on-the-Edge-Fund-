**What happened.** A US–Iran ceasefire in April took oil off its peak, earnings season confirmed AI demand, and chip stocks went parabolic: the Philadelphia semiconductor index was up about 55% for the year by early May and the Nasdaq set repeated records, with Micron joining the $1 trillion club in late May.

**Why the fund moved like this.** This is the rebound I expected in March. In April the fund simply tracked QQQ; in May it outperformed sharply as the high-beta AI and space names caught up. The fund reached its all-time high at the end of May.

**What I expected next.** I became more cautious, for two reasons. Inflation was re-accelerating (oil still above $100, the Fed under a new chair, Kevin Warsh, with rate cuts off the table) and positioning in high-beta momentum was extreme. My base case was a pull-back in the most crowded names over the summer, not a change in the long-term thesis.

**Multi-asset view.** Take profits on the oil hedge after the ceasefire, keep gold as the core diversifier even though it had started to correct, and stay away from long-duration bonds with inflation rising.
