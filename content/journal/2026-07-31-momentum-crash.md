**What happened.** A strong June jobs report and sticky inflation pushed the Fed towards hiking: by mid-June nearly half of policymakers projected a hike in 2026. Bitcoin fell below $60,000, more than 54% below its peak. In July the chip index lost about 18% after doubling in the first half, a Chinese AI lab released a model said to rival leading US systems, and the Nasdaq-100 entered a correction on 28 July. Goldman Sachs' high-beta momentum basket fell about 37% in July alone.

**Why the fund moved like this.** This is the clearest example of the fund's factor exposure: it fell more than three times as much as QQQ over two months. The book is essentially long high-beta momentum, so when that factor unwound, stock selection could not offset it.

**What I expected next.** Factor crashes like this usually exhaust themselves once positioning is cleared, and nothing in the company news flow justified the size of the move. I expected stabilisation rather than a V-shaped recovery, with the Fed as the key swing factor.

**Multi-asset view.** Cash and T-bills were the best hedge with policy rates near 3.6% and a hike priced in. Gold had dipped below $4,000/oz in late June, which I would have used to rebuild the position. This period is the strongest argument for the risk rules I describe in *About*: a speculative cap and risk-based position limits.
