**What happened.** After the tariff pause, markets went straight back to the AI trade: hyperscaler capex guidance kept rising, the Fed started cutting rates in September, and retail and crypto activity boomed, with bitcoin peaking near $126,000 in early October.

**Why the fund moved like this.** This is the period that built the track record: the fund roughly doubled while QQQ gained about a third. Beta explains part of it, but the gap is far larger than beta alone would give. The winners were exactly the conviction names: GE Vernova (power for data centres), Rocket Lab, Palantir and Robinhood. This is where most of the positive selection P&L comes from.

**What I expected next.** By October the risk/reward had changed. Weights had drifted heavily towards the winners and valuations were stretched. My expectation was a correction led by the most speculative names, and the 10 October crypto liquidation (around $19bn of positions wiped out in a day) was a warning sign for COIN and MARA. Under the no-rebalancing rule I let it run; under today's rules I would have trimmed back to the bucket targets.

**Multi-asset view.** Gold kept rising (above $4,000/oz in October), so I would have kept the gold allocation rather than sell it to chase equities, and started adding duration as the Fed began to cut.
