**What happened.** November 2025 brought the first serious "AI bubble" debate: investors questioned circular financing between AI labs and chip makers and the return on record capex. The S&P 500 fell more than 5% from its late-October record, and bitcoin kept sliding after the October liquidation.

**Why the fund moved like this.** The fund lost far more than QQQ in November, because it owned both ends of the risk-off trade: the AI momentum names that had run the most, and the crypto proxies (COIN, MARA, HOOD). December partly reversed it as high-beta names rebounded while QQQ was flat.

**What I expected next.** I read November as a positioning flush, not a change in fundamentals: AI demand and backlog data were still accelerating. I expected the AI infrastructure names to recover and the crypto names to stay weak, since crypto had lost its momentum bid.

**Multi-asset view.** Bonds started to work again as the Fed kept cutting into December; gold ended 2025 up by more than 60%, its best year since 1979. A 60/40-style overlay would have softened this drawdown materially.
