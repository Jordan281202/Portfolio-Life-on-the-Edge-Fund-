## SpaceX (SPCX)
**Why.** SpaceX listed on the Nasdaq on 11 June 2026 at $135 a share, targeting a valuation of about $1.77 trillion. It dominates global launch, owns the largest satellite constellation (Starlink) and, since acquiring xAI in February 2026, also gives exposure to frontier AI. It is the natural core holding of the space theme that Rocket Lab currently carries alone.

**Position.** 5–7% of the book, funded by closing MARA and cutting COIN, which would lower the portfolio's risk while keeping the thematic exposure.

**Risks.** Valuation at listing, key-person and governance risk, and the xAI merger making the investment case harder to value.

## Anthropic (pre-IPO)
**Why.** One of the two leading frontier AI labs, with fast enterprise adoption of its models and coding tools. It raised $65 billion in June 2026 at a $965 billion valuation and has filed confidentially for an IPO, reportedly seeking a valuation of around $1.4 trillion. Owning a model maker would complete the AI stack in the book (chips with Nvidia, power with GE Vernova, applications with Palantir).

**Position.** A starter position of 3–4% at the IPO, built up only after the first quarterly results as a listed company.

**Risks.** Valuation, very high compute costs and cash burn, competition from other labs and open-weight models, and an IPO coming at a volatile moment for AI stocks.
